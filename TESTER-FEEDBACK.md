## Useful details

For fleet or cargo problems, include the group type, equipment, available stock
and the quantity you tried to move. For an unexpected attack, include the
attacker and planet shown in the notice.

For cinematic or audio problems, describe where playback stopped or went wrong,
whether you paused or left the screen, and what happened when you returned.

A named save made just before a problem is useful. Keep it locally and mention
that you have it in your report. For startup failures, check `logs/launch.log`
when running a Windows package, or the terminal output when running from source.
Remove personal paths and original game text before sharing log excerpts.
Don't attach original game files or converted assets.

When continuing from a supplied checkpoint, save under a new name and include
the checkpoint's exact filename in your report.
